# Outline

In my final project I want to show why the temporary protected status for Haitians in America should be reinstated. I want highlight why the TPS  was given, which was because of Haiti's 2010 earthquake that displaced many people. Haitians were able to go to America find refuge whilst being able to work and live here legally. Since 2010 the conditions of Haiti has only worsened with gang territories growing and taking control of many areas. From a corrupt government, high level of poverty, massacres, other violence's, according to the department of state Haiti is on a level 4 travel ban. Under the new administration, TPS for Haitian has been terminated and beneficiaries are subjected to deporation.

From August to September there has been a little over 200 people deported back to Haiti. Using data, I want to show the impact of Haitians in America. For instance, how the contribute to the economy and what they've done to advance our society. I also want to highlight the crime rate in Haiti over time and show that the living conditions there are volatile. There is a need for continued TPS and sending Haitians back to Haiti at this time is sending them to their deaths. My audience will be grassroot organization and other communities who will rally and push for congress to reinstate TPS. We will mobilize communities to call their state reps and write to their congresswoman to fight for reinstatement. I wanted to end on a middle note. Not sad but not so happy either. There is a caveat to all of this. Even if TPS were to get reinstated, there is still a question of for how long. So, while we are pushing for reinstatement, we should also push further for the support of Haitian Refugee Immigration Fairness Act of 2026. This will create a pathway to lawful permanent residency for these TPS holders.  

Outline structure

-I will start the project on a high note, praising  TPS coverage and what it did for thousands of Haitians in the U.S and in Haiti. I want to show how many people became tps holders with data viz.

-I will then highlight contributions to our economy and communities that Haitian TPS holder have made, with data showcasing these positive contributions. 
-Next I will  touch on Haiti's humanitarian issue and trends from 2010-2026. This will highlight rise in crime, economic disparities, and deteriorating living conditions. Will use a data viz, maybe a line graph showing rises and spikes. 

-I want to then present the new rulings for tps and what it means for the beneficiaries. Specfically show some form of  data viz that shows how many Haitian immigrants are affected. (this is the portion of the story that is in the red/climax)
-I want to heavily emphasis on deportation numbers and highlight a recent massacre of 50 people in a town in Haiti. I want to frame this as a humanitarian ethical issue. (if U.S doesn't want their citizen to travel to Haiti, why is it okay to send tps beneficiaries.) I will tie this back to why tps should be reinstated. (safety of human beings)

-This last part will be a "turning point with a caveat." It will be a call of action to grassroot orgs and communities to go/write to their reps/congress and rally for the reinstatement of tps. However, I want to leave the audience with the fact that the greater work is to be done in pushing for the Haitian Refugee 
Immigration Fairness Act of 2026. 

I hope to inspire the audience to want to be apart of this story. Making an issue that has nothing to do with U.S Americans important to them is difficult. However, if I could frame it as a moral duty of all Americas to fight for the safety of Haitian immigrants. I hope to stir up a fire that pushes people to want to think how can I help. 

My story Arc:

As a grassroot organization/ community I want to know about why tps reinstatement  matters on a high level, so that I can do my part in making sure that it happens. I can do this by organizing rally's, community gatherings, writing to my rep/congress to highlight the issues with the tps termination/deportation, current unlivable conditions in Haiti and why tps should be reinstated.
