Since 2010 the conditions of Haiti has only worsened with gang territories growing and taking control of many areas. For instance, how the contribute to the economy and what they've done to advance our society. I also want to highlight the crime rate in Haiti over time and show that the living conditions there are volatile. There is a need for continued TPS and sending Haitians back to Haiti at this time is sending them to their deaths.
