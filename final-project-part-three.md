# Final thoughts

I think overall this data's story went okay. I think I could better explain the importance of this issue and make it more relatable to a person who isn't Haitian. I wish I could have talked more about the Haitian Refugee Immigration Fairness Act, which talks about creating a pathway to residency for Haitian nationals to live here permanently. TPS has many facets, so I tried to choose a part that told they story of why its really needed. The greater story though, is the thousands of Haitian nationals living in limbo because of TPS. TPS actually gets renewed every year, and you have to reapply each time to get it. While TPS offers protections, its a constant reminder that you are still a foreign in the U.S. Haitians are American enough to contribute economically but not enough to vote. I can go on and on. Th important thing is awareness of tps and a pathway to residency for Haitian needs to be highlighted more. This isn't a political issue but its a moral obligation. I was very excited to share information on something that I hold dear. I really hope this makes a difference, even if it doesn't go anywhere I hope the conversation did something. I think my biggest question in doing this assignment was "how do I make people not at all affected by this issue care?" Theres room for growth for me, but I hope in the future I can tell and use data viz to tell this story more effectively.
